- Editor viewports take mouse and keyboard input: holding the right mouse button over a viewport
  flies its camera with W/A/S/D and Q/E (by key position, on any layout) in relative mouse mode,
  and the cursor returns to where it started when the button is released; the wheel changes the
  fly speed meanwhile. Alt + left drag orbits, Alt + right drag dollies, a middle drag pans and the
  wheel dollies, at the same speed on any display scale. Without the right button W, E, R and the
  other shortcuts keep working, and Ctrl and Alt shortcuts such as Ctrl + Z work during flight too.
  A camera drag that leaves the viewport keeps going until the button is released, without
  clicking or scrolling the panels it passes over.
