- `ith/reflection`: `ith::typeOf<T>()` and `ith::TypeRegistry` describe reflected types with
  stable FNV-1a ids, field offsets and types, construct, copy, move, destroy and field-wise
  equality, flags and attributes, and find types by id, name or `FormerName`. Shipping builds
  compile out tooltips, categories, display names and `Meta`. See
  `docs/development/reflection.md`.
